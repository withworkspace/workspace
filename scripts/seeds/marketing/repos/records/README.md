# records

What Sorrel has decided, what it's proposing, and what's worth keeping. Shared through main.

- `adr/NNNN-<short-name>.md`: a decision, numbered from 0001. Context, decision, consequences.
- `proposals/<short-name>.md`: a change to what customers see. The problem, the proposal, and what changes.
- `notes/<short-name>.md`: anything worth keeping that isn't a decision.
