title: Tint the app with your accent, or take dark to black
link: /settings/appearance
picture: accent-tint.webp
---
Two new sliders in **Settings › Appearance**. **Tint** carries your accent
into the greys, from the page to the panels, without changing contrast.
**Depth** takes the dark theme down to true black, for OLED screens, and is
kept per browser.
