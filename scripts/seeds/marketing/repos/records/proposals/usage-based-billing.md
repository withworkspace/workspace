# Usage-based billing

## Problem

Studios that bill by the hour build each invoice by hand from a timesheet. The hours are already somewhere else.

## Proposal

A line can take its quantity from a meter: hours from a time tracker, seats from an identity provider. The invoice is drafted at the end of the period with what the meter says, and nothing is sent until someone has read it.

## Open questions

- Which meters first. Time tracking is most of the asks.
- What a meter that's down does to a draft.
