It's worth proposing, and it isn't small.

**What's in the way.** A sent invoice is frozen (ADR 0003), so a fee can't be added to the invoice it's for. It would go out as an invoice of its own: a new number, a new PDF, and one more thing for a customer to pay.

**What's already decided.** The reminders proposal left late fees out on purpose: "that's a decision about money, and a proposal of its own". Reminders are being built now, in `reminders-for-overdue-invoices`, and a fee would run from the same daily job.

**What I'd do.** Write the proposal first, as the process asks for anything customers see. I've proposed a mission for that below. It builds nothing.

One thing to settle before then is whether it's a flat fee or interest by the day. Interest changes every day until it's paid, so the proposal would have to say when it's invoiced.
